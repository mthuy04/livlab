## Display policy

- Exact references display “Giá tham khảo ngoài” and the unit.
- Category-level references never inherit an exact SKU claim.
- Detail pages expose source name/link and date checked.
- Quote context preserves price classification and `isOfficialShowroomOffer`.
- Unknown prices display “Liên hệ báo giá”. A mixed basket must not imply that its reference subtotal is a complete formal quote.

## Refresh policy

Re-check sources before production launch and on a scheduled commercial-data review. If a page no longer supports the figure, remove the reference until it is reverified; do not silently carry forward stale values.
